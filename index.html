ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Delivering Dependable Water Solutions for Every Project

EBICH Tech Drills Limited is an engineering and water solutions company based in Kuje, Abuja, Nigeria, with 10 years of experience in borehole drilling and water engineering.

We specialize in professional borehole drilling, geophysical surveys, groundwater exploration, borehole installation, pump installation, plumbing, solar-powered borehole systems, and borehole maintenance.

Over the years, we have focused on providing reliable and sustainable water solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial properties. From identifying suitable groundwater locations through geophysical surveys to drilling, installation, and maintenance, we provide comprehensive borehole services tailored to our clients’ needs.

Our commitment is to deliver quality workmanship, dependable water supply solutions, professional service, and customer satisfaction.
